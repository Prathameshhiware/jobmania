# Directory listings and backlinks

Copy for submitting JoBmania to directories. Everything here is checked
against the live database on 9 October 2026 — update the figures before
pasting if it has been a while, because a listing that overstates is the one
thing this site cannot afford to do.

## Why this is worth doing at all

Not for ranking. Almost every directory link is `nofollow`, so it passes no
authority. It is worth doing because Search Console shows the real problem:
40 crawl requests in ninety days, 100% of them refreshing pages Google
already had, and **zero discovery crawls**. 705 pages sit "Discovered –
currently not indexed".

A nofollow link on a page Google crawls daily still gives Googlebot a path
here. That is what is missing, and it is why three good listings beat thirty
bad ones.

Do not submit to bulk directory lists. Google treats that as a link scheme,
and the downside is larger than the upside.

### Name

https://jobmania.dpdns.org

### Tagline, 60 characters
Job openings in India that are still actually open

### One line, 140 characters
A job board for India that removes listings when they close. Every apply

### Short, around 300 characters
JoBmania lists job openings in India that are still open. Every listing is
read from the employer's own careers page, every apply link is re-tested
through the day, and anything that closes is taken down rather than left up
to collect applications nobody reads. Free, no account, no fee.

### Long, around 900 characters
Most job sites are judged on how many listings they show. JoBmania is built
on the opposite idea: a listing is only useful if the role is still open, the
apply link still works, and the employer wrote the description.

Openings are read directly from employers' own careers systems, never
rewritten and never re-dated. Apply links are re-tested through the day, and
a role that closes is removed rather than left up. 341 listings have been
taken down so far, each published with its reason, so the claim can be
checked rather than taken on trust.

Every application goes to the employer's own page. Nothing routes through us,
there is no account to create, and no listing will ever ask a candidate for
money — the single most common way job seekers in India are defrauded.

609 openings from 211 employers, including 217 open to freshers, 60 walk-in
drives and 49 internships.

## Where to submit, in order of what it is worth

### 1. Instagram bio — free, immediate, already yours
@jobmania.in exists. If the bio does not carry the link, that is the fastest
backlink available and it costs nothing.

### 2. Product Hunt — free, high authority, real traffic
A genuine launch, not a directory drop. Worth preparing properly: the logo is
in `brand/`, and the carousel cards in `.preview-cards/` work as screenshots.
Launch on a weekday morning IST.

### 3. Crunchbase — free company profile
High authority and indexed quickly. Use the long description above.

### 4. F6S — free startup profile
Lower authority than the two above, but free and takes five minutes.

### 5. AlternativeTo — list as an alternative to Naukri and Indeed
Honest positioning: smaller, verified, no account required. Their 403 to
scripts is bot protection, the site is live in a browser.

### Not worth it
SaaSHub — this is not SaaS. BetaList — for pre-launch products, this is live.
Any site offering "500 directory submissions" — that is the link scheme
Google penalises.

## Worth more than any directory

One genuinely useful answer on Reddit (r/developersIndia, r/IndianWorkplace)
or Quora, where someone is actually asking how to find verified openings.
Read the subreddit rules first; both treat self-promotion harshly, and a
removed post helps nobody.

The honest pitch is the removals page: a job site that publishes what it took
down, and why, is unusual enough to be worth mentioning on its own.
