If you have been told there is no point chasing AI jobs without a machine learning degree, that advice is out of date.

The foundit Insights Tracker for July 2026 found AI linked roles have gone from roughly **1 per cent to 4 per cent** of fresher hiring in India in a year. The more useful number is the second one: **51 per cent of those roles are AI operations**, not model development.

Just over half of the AI opportunity for freshers is work that does not require you to have trained a model.

## What AI operations actually means

The phrase sounds vague, so here is the concrete version. An AI system is only as good as the data going in and the checking happening around it. That checking is a job, and in India it is increasingly an entry level one.

It covers:

- Preparing, cleaning and labelling the data a model learns from
- Writing and refining prompts so a system returns what it is supposed to
- Evaluating outputs against guidelines and flagging where they are wrong
- Testing a system for failures before and after it goes live
- Monitoring behaviour once the thing is running at scale
- Writing the documentation and guidelines that other reviewers follow

None of that requires building a model. All of it requires someone who reads carefully, handles data without making mistakes, and can explain why an output is wrong.

## Why this is the realistic door

Everyone applies to the job titled AI Engineer. Far fewer apply to the one titled Data Associate, Quality Analyst, AI Evaluator or Operations Associate, because those titles do not sound like AI jobs.

They are the same market. The applicant pool for the second set is thinner, and a fresher competing there is competing against far fewer people holding specialist degrees.

There is supporting evidence in what employers say they want. TeamLease EdTech's Career Outlook Report for July to December 2026 lists **Data Centre Operations Associate** among the five most in demand fresher roles of the half year, alongside AI Application Developer. Three of its five named roles are operations roles.

## Where these jobs are

Overwhelmingly at Global Capability Centres, which are the India offices global companies own and run themselves rather than outsourcing.

According to the same foundit tracker, GCCs raised their share of entry level hiring in India from **8 per cent to 11 per cent** in a year, the largest gain of any employer category, while traditional IT services firms fell from **32 per cent to 24 per cent**. They expanded across AI, engineering, analytics and finance operations.

Bengaluru and Hyderabad are the main hubs, with Delhi NCR, Pune, Chennai and Mumbai behind them. If you want the full version of how to approach them, we wrote a [guide to getting into a GCC as a fresher](/insights/playbook/how-to-get-into-a-gcc-as-a-fresher).

## What to actually learn

This is where most advice sends people in the wrong direction. If the job is evaluation and data work, then learning to train a neural network from scratch is not the highest value use of your next three months.

What these roles reward:

**Working with data without errors.** Spreadsheets properly, SQL if you can, and the discipline to not introduce mistakes into a dataset.

**Clear written English.** Much of this work is writing prompts, writing guidelines, and writing down why an output was marked wrong. It is a writing job more than people expect.

**Judgement in a specific domain.** Healthcare, finance, retail, law. Knowing what a correct answer looks like in a field is the thing a model cannot do for itself, and it is where a non engineering graduate has a genuine advantage.

**Attention to detail.** The entire job is noticing that something is subtly wrong. People who rush are bad at it regardless of qualifications.

## Be realistic about the size of it

Four per cent of fresher hiring is not a lot. AI roles are growing fast in percentage terms from a small base, and the headlines run well ahead of the hiring.

So treat this as one channel, not a plan. Apply to AI operations roles because the competition is thinner, while also applying to the parts of the market that are larger right now. [Festive and seasonal hiring](/insights/blogs/festive-season-jobs-2026-india) is adding up to 2.5 lakh roles before December. Our [live openings](/jobs) and [fresher jobs](/c/freshers) pages show what is actually open today.

It is also worth knowing why this channel matters more than it used to. IT fresher hiring fell roughly 80 per cent between FY22 and FY25, which we covered in [IT fresher hiring fell 80 per cent](/insights/blogs/it-fresher-hiring-india-2026). The traditional entry point into Indian tech narrowed sharply. AI operations is one of the few entry points that widened.

## The short version

AI hiring for freshers is real but small, at about 4 per cent of the market. More than half of it is operations work, which does not need a specialist degree and attracts fewer applicants precisely because the job titles do not say AI. That combination is the opportunity.
