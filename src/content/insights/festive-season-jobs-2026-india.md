If you are looking for work right now, the next eight weeks are the busiest hiring window India sees all year.

Staffing firm NLB Services expects the country to add **2 to 2.5 lakh seasonal and gig jobs** between July and December 2026. Festive hiring is set to rise 15 to 20 per cent on last year. Most of these roles are entry level, most do not ask for experience, and a large share of them sit outside Mumbai, Delhi and Bengaluru.

Here is what the numbers actually say, and what to do about them.

## Where the growth is: it is not the metros

This is the part most people get wrong.

Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities are expected to account for around **45 per cent of total festive hiring demand**, and hiring in those markets is projected to grow **25 to 30 per cent**. That growth rate is higher than the national average, which means your odds are better in a smaller city than in a metro this season.

The report names these cities specifically:

| City | State |
| --- | --- |
| Jaipur | Rajasthan |
| Lucknow | Uttar Pradesh |
| Indore | Madhya Pradesh |
| Surat | Gujarat |
| Nagpur | Maharashtra |
| Bhubaneswar | Odisha |
| Coimbatore | Tamil Nadu |
| Chandigarh | Chandigarh |
| Kochi | Kerala |

What is driving it is simple. E-commerce, quick commerce and organised retail have been building warehouses and dark stores in these cities for the last two years. Those buildings need staff, and they need them before Diwali rather than after.

## Which jobs are actually opening

The bulk of festive hiring goes to roles that have existed for years:

- Last mile delivery
- Warehousing and fulfilment
- Retail sales
- Customer experience and support
- Inventory management
- Field operations
- Hospitality

If you have worked in a [customer support or voice process role](/c/freshers) before, you are already qualified for a large share of what is being advertised.

There is also a newer set of roles growing 15 to 20 per cent, and these are worth knowing because fewer people apply to them:

- Dark store inventory controllers
- Returns and refunds specialists
- Customer resolution executives
- Marketplace operations
- Catalogue managers
- Order management specialists
- Fraud and risk personnel

These titles did not exist five years ago. They came out of quick commerce, and because the job titles are unfamiliar, the applicant pool for them is smaller than for a delivery or retail role.

## The quick commerce piece, stated accurately

You will see headlines claiming quick commerce accounts for almost half of all festive jobs. That is not what the report says, and it is worth being precise.

What NLB Services actually projects is that **gig worker demand will grow by 20 per cent**, and that **45 per cent of that gig share** comes from quick commerce. Gig work is one slice of festive hiring, not the whole thing. Quick commerce is a large part of that slice.

It still matters. Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart and their competitors run dark stores in dozens of cities now, and each one needs pickers, packers, inventory staff and delivery riders through the festive peak. But if you are reading a headline that says quick commerce is half of all festive hiring, that headline is wrong.

## Apply now, not during the festival

This is the single most useful thing in the report.

Companies begin festive recruitment **12 to 14 weeks before the peak period**. Hiring is not something that happens during Diwali. It happens well before it, because staff need to be onboarded and trained in time.

Counting back from the festive peak, that window is open right now. If you wait until the week of the festival to start applying, the roles will already be filled.

Two practical consequences:

1. Apply this month, not next month.
2. Apply to several employers at once. Seasonal hiring moves fast and quietly, and a role that is open on Monday is often gone by Friday.

You can see what is live today on our [all jobs page](/jobs), and [walk-in interviews](/c/walk-ins) are worth watching closely in this period because seasonal hiring tends to run through walk-in drives rather than long online processes.

## Before you accept a seasonal job

Three things to check, because the festive rush is also the season when fake job offers rise.

**Never pay anything.** No real employer charges a registration fee, a security deposit, or a training charge for a seasonal role. If money is being asked for, it is not a job.

**Get the contract length in writing.** A festive role is temporary by design. Ask how many weeks or months it runs for, and ask whether the company converts seasonal staff to permanent, before you accept.

**Confirm the venue before you travel.** If it is a walk-in, call the number on the employer's own website rather than a number sent to you on WhatsApp. We keep a public record of every listing we have [taken down and why](/closed), and dead or changed venues are a common reason.

## The short version

India is adding up to 2.5 lakh seasonal roles before December. Nearly half the demand is in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities, where hiring is growing faster than in the metros. Most roles are entry level. The hiring window is open now and closes before the festivals, not during them.
