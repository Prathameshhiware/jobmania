Fake job postings are not a rare problem in India. They are an industry, and freshers are the target, because someone applying for their first job has no sense of what is normal.

This guide gives you the checks that expose a fake in under two minutes, and explains what a genuinely **trusted job site** should be willing to show you about itself.

## The one rule that needs no experience

**No genuine employer in India asks a candidate for money.** Not a registration fee. Not a security deposit. Not a training charge. Not a kit cost. Not a "fully refundable" amount you will supposedly get back after joining.

That is the whole rule. You do not need to judge whether the company looks real, whether the office address checks out, or whether the recruiter sounds professional. The moment money is requested, it is over. Close the chat.

Scammers work hard to make the request sound reasonable: a one-time documentation charge, a laptop deposit, a certification you must buy first. It does not matter how it is framed.

## Six signs of a fake job posting

**The salary is far above the role.** Fifty thousand a month for a fresher data entry job is not a lucky break.

**The company name is vague.** "A leading MNC" with no name. Real employers want their name on their own openings.

**The email is personal, not corporate.** A recruiter writing from a gmail or outlook address for a company that has its own domain.

**You are rushed.** "Confirm within two hours or the seat goes." Urgency is how people are stopped from thinking.

**The whole interview happens over chat.** No call, no video, no office. Just messages, and then a payment request.

**There is no job description.** A real posting explains what you will do. A fake one describes the salary.

Any two of these together is enough to walk away.

Most **legit job sites** ask you to trust them. Very few give you a way to check. These are the four things a site should be able to prove.

### The apply link goes to the employer

Of the **623 openings** live today, **zero** keep you on our site. Every application happens on the employer's own page. A site that routes you through itself is managing you, not helping you.

### The posting date is honest

Some sites re-date old postings so the feed looks active. You apply to something advertised as today's that was published in August.

We never alter a date. If a role went up on 14 August, the listing says 14 August.

JoBmania is free, with no account to create. We will never ask you for money, and no listing on the site will either. Our footer carries this warning on every page, because it is the single most useful sentence we can give a first-time job seeker.

## If you have already been asked for money

Stop replying. Do not send anything further, even to "get your deposit back", which is a common second stage. Keep screenshots of the chat, the posting and any payment. Report it to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at **cybercrime.gov.in** or call **1930**, the national cyber fraud helpline. If you paid by UPI or bank transfer, tell your bank immediately, because speed genuinely matters.
