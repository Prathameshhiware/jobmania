Search for the **best Indian job portals** and you will find a dozen listicles, most of them written by someone with something to sell. This one is written by a job site, so treat it with the same suspicion, and then check the claims. We have made that possible on purpose.

## How to judge the best Indian job portals: four tests

Forget listing counts. Any **job portal website** can inflate those. Here is what actually determines whether a portal gets you a job.

### 1. Does it remove listings when they close?

This is the big one. Most **job portals** have no process for taking a posting down. It sits there for months, collecting applications nobody reads.

We remove them. Anything past its closing date comes off within **30 minutes**, and every apply link is re-tested about **1,900 times a day**. **246 listings have been removed so far**, and each one is published with its reason on our [Recently closed](/closed) page. That page exists so you can audit the claim rather than take our word for it.

### 2. Does the apply link go to the employer?

Open a listing on any **job search site** and click apply. If you land on another page of the same site, you are inside a funnel designed to keep you there.

Of the **623 openings** on JoBmania today, **zero** route you through anyone. Every link goes to the employer's own application page.

### 3. Is the posted date honest?

Some sites re-date old postings so the feed looks fresh. You apply to something advertised as posted today that went up in August.

We never do this. If a role was published on 14 August, the listing says 14 August, even though a fresher date would get us more clicks.

### 4. Does it ever ask you for money?

A registration fee, a security deposit, a training charge, a "refundable" amount. Any of these, from any site or any recruiter, is the end of the conversation. No genuine employer in India asks a candidate for money. We are free, with no account to create.
