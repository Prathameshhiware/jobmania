There is a point in every job search where you realise the problem is not your CV. You are applying to the same postings as ten thousand other people, through the same two or three sites everyone uses.

The way out is not to apply harder. It is to look where fewer people are looking. This is a guide to **job search platforms** and **job boards** beyond the obvious ones.

## Why crowded portals stop working

A posting on a mass-market **job platform** can collect thousands of applications in a day. Nobody reads a thousand CVs. In practice a recruiter opens the first forty and stops.

So two things decide whether you are seen, and neither is talent: **how early you applied**, and **how many others saw the same posting**.

That second number is the one you can actually change.

## Job search platforms where the openings are less crowded

### Employers' own hiring systems

Most companies publish a role on their own system first. It may reach the big **job posting sites** days later, or never. Anyone reading the company's system directly sees it first and competes with far fewer people.

That is most of what JoBmania does. We read employers' own hiring systems, so a role arrives here with the company's own description, the company's real posting date, and a link straight to their application form. Of the **623 openings** live today, **every single one** sends you to the employer rather than through us.

### Walk-in interviews

Underrated, and almost invisible on **job search engines**. You turn up, interview that day, and often hear back the same week. There is no shortlisting queue to sit in. **46 walk-in drives** are live now on our [walk-ins page](/c/walk-ins), each with its date, timing and venue exactly as the employer stated them.

### Off-campus drives

If placements did not work out, off-campus drives are the same opportunity without the college gate. **32 are open** on the [off-campus page](/c/off-campus).

### Internships that convert

**53 internships** are live. For anyone stuck in the no-experience loop, this remains the most reliable door, and it is listed separately on the [internships page](/c/internships) so you are not scrolling past senior roles to find them.

## Remote and work from home, carefully

**Remote job boards** and **work from home websites** are the most useful category for anyone outside the big cities, and also the one where scams concentrate.

**44 work from home openings** are live on our [remote page](/c/remote). The rule that protects you is the same everywhere: no genuine remote employer asks you to pay for training, a starter kit, registration or a deposit. Not before joining, not after. If money is requested at any point, that is the entire answer about the offer.

Everything on JoBmania is checked against the employer's own posting before it goes live, and every removal is published with its reason on our [Recently closed](/closed) page.

## A practical routine across these job search platforms

1. **Check [Just Posted](/c/just-posted) every morning.** The first 48 hours is where the odds are.
2. **Pick a less crowded route.** A walk-in on Thursday beats your sixtieth online application.
3. **Apply to five properly, not fifty carelessly.** Read the description, match two lines of your CV to it.
4. **Go direct wherever you can.** If you see a role here, you are already applying on the employer's own page.
5. **Never pay anyone.** Not once, not for anything.
