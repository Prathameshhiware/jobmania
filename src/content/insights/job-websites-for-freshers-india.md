If you are a fresher in India, you already know the problem. You open a job site, you see hundreds of openings, you apply to forty of them, and you hear nothing back. The site was not lying to you exactly. Most of those openings had already closed.

This guide is about finding **job websites for freshers** that are worth your time, and about the three routes that actually hire people with no experience.

## What makes job websites for freshers useful

Most **job sites for freshers** are judged on how many listings they show. That is the wrong number to care about. A listing helps you only if three things are true:

1. The role is still open.
2. The apply link still works.
3. The employer wrote the description, so you know what the job actually involves.

A site that shows you 50,000 openings and fails all three is worse than a site that shows you 600 and passes all three. You only have so many evenings.

We built JoBmania around that idea. Right now there are **623 openings live**, from **207 different employers**, and **212 of them are open to freshers or to people with up to two years of experience**. Every one of those apply links was tested within the last 24 hours.

## Three routes that job websites for freshers rarely separate

Here is the part most **job finding websites** never explain. Employers hire freshers through three doors, and they are not the same door.

### Walk-in interviews

You turn up, you interview that day, and many people get an offer the same week. No shortlisting, no waiting for a callback. This is the fastest route into a first job in India, and it is why **46 walk-in drives** are live on JoBmania right now.

The risk is that drives get moved and cancelled at short notice, and nothing wastes a day like travelling across a city to a venue where nothing is happening. Always confirm on the employer's own page before you travel. Every [walk-in interview](/c/walk-ins) we list carries the date, the timing and the venue exactly as the employer stated them, and drives come off the site the day they finish.

### Internships

An internship converts into a full-time role far more often than a cold application does, and it is the honest answer to "how do I get experience when every job needs experience". There are **53 internships** live now. Browse them on the [internships page](/c/internships).

### Off-campus drives

If your college placement cell did not come through, off-campus drives are the same thing without the college. **32 off-campus drives** are currently open on [this page](/c/off-campus).

## How to get noticed by startups

Startups rarely post on the big **job portals**. They post on their own careers page, and that is the end of it. If you are only looking at **job search sites** that aggregate the mass market, you will never see those roles.

JoBmania reads employers' own hiring systems directly. That means a startup role arrives here with the company's own job description, the company's own posting date, and a link that takes you straight to the company's application form rather than to a middleman.

A practical tip that costs nothing: apply within the first 48 hours. Startup teams are small and they often stop reading once they have a shortlist. Our [Just Posted](/c/just-posted) page exists for exactly this.

## What we do differently, in plain terms

We are not going to claim we have the most listings in India. We do not. Here is what we actually do, and you can check every one of these:

- **Every apply link is tested, roughly 1,900 times a day.** If a link starts returning an error, the listing comes off the site.
- **We remove closed jobs instead of leaving them up.** 246 listings have been taken down so far. Each one is published with its reason on our [Recently closed](/closed) page, so you can check the claim rather than trust it.
- **Every apply link goes to the employer.** Not to us, not to a third party. Of the 623 openings live today, **zero** route you through an intermediary.
- **69 per cent carry the employer's own job description**, not a rewritten summary.
- **Real posted dates.** We never re-date an old posting to make it look new. If a job was posted on 14 August, it says 14 August.
- **No registration fee, ever.** Not for you, not for anyone.

That last point deserves a line of its own. No legitimate employer or job site in India asks a candidate for money. Not a registration fee, not a security deposit, not a training charge, not a "refundable" amount. If anyone asks, that is the whole answer about them.

## A realistic plan for your week

1. Pick your city page and your category. If you are in Hyderabad and want a walk-in, that is [walk-ins in Hyderabad](/city/hyderabad/walk-ins).
2. Apply to five roles properly rather than fifty roles carelessly. Read the description. Match two or three lines of your CV to it.

4. For walk-ins, confirm the venue on the employer's page the night before.
5. Keep a simple list of where you applied and when, so you know who to follow up with.
