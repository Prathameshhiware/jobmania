If you are hunting for GCC jobs in India as a fresher, you are looking at the fastest growing part of the entry level market. If you are applying to the same six IT services companies as everyone in your batch, you are competing in the part that is shrinking.

Here is the shift, in one pair of numbers. Over the year to July 2026, Global Capability Centres raised their share of entry-level hiring in India from 8% to 11%, the biggest gain of any employer category. Over the same period, traditional IT services firms fell from 32% to 24%. That is from the foundit Insights Tracker, reported by *Business Today* in August 2026.

Nothing about that guarantees you a job. But it tells you where to spend your applications.

## What is a GCC (Global Capability Centre)?

A Global Capability Centre is an office that a foreign company owns and staffs itself in India, instead of paying an Indian IT services vendor to do that work for it.

The distinction matters to you for one reason: you are hired by the company, not by a supplier. You work on that company's own products and systems, your appraisal happens inside that company, and your next role is an internal move rather than a change of employer.

You already know these names. Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Goldman Sachs, Deutsche Bank, SAP, Salesforce, Visa, Bosch, Siemens, Honeywell and Qualcomm all run large centres here. They are not obscure. They are simply not the companies that come to most campuses.

## GCC jobs in Bengaluru, Hyderabad and other cities

Most GCC jobs in India sit in two cities: Bengaluru and Hyderabad, with Delhi NCR, Pune, Chennai and Mumbai behind them.

On fresher hiring across all employers, the same tracker puts Delhi NCR first at 19%, with Bengaluru, Mumbai and Pune at 14% each. TeamLease EdTech's *Career Outlook Report* for July to December 2026 has Bangalore leading on hiring intent at 89%, Mumbai at 75% and Chennai at 71%.

If you can relocate, relocating to Bengaluru or Hyderabad is the single highest leverage thing on this page. If you cannot, apply to the centres that hire remotely and be honest with yourself that the pool is smaller.

As of 29 September 2026, 33 of the 209 live openings on JoBmania are at companies that run GCCs in India: Accenture, Qualcomm, Amazon, IBM, Siemens, Oracle, SAP, Salesforce, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Google, Goldman Sachs, Deutsche Bank, Visa, Honeywell and Bosch. Most are in [Hyderabad](/city/hyderabad) and [Bengaluru](/city/bengaluru).

## Which GCC jobs can a fresher actually get?

This is where most advice fails people. "AI jobs" sounds like it means building models. Overwhelmingly, it does not.

The foundit tracker found AI-linked roles have gone from about 1% to 4% of fresher hiring, and that 51% of those are AI operations: data analysis, prompt engineering, evaluation and testing. Just over half of the AI opportunity is work that does not require you to have trained a model.

That is the door. Not "AI Engineer", but the operations, analysis and evaluation roles sitting next to it.

TeamLease's report names Data Centre Operations Associate among the five most in-demand fresher roles of this half-year, alongside AI Application Developer, Jr. Semiconductor Design Engineer, Programmatic Advertising Associate and Inventory & Dispatch Executive. Three of those five are operations roles.

Two more things worth knowing from the same reports. Healthcare was the fastest growing function for freshers, rising from 7% to 11%. And overall fresher hiring fell 10% year on year even as technology hiring grew 10%, so the market is not uniformly good. It is moving.

## How to apply for GCC jobs in India

Apply on the company's own careers site. A GCC posts its openings on its own domain. Job aggregators, this one included, are a discovery layer, useful for finding out that a role exists, but the application belongs on the employer's page. If a listing anywhere sends you somewhere other than the company's own site to apply, slow down.

Filter for India and your city, not for job titles you already know. GCC role titles rarely match campus vocabulary. Searching "Operations Associate" or "Analyst" in Hyderabad will show you more than searching "Software Engineer" will.

Apply to the operations and analyst roles, not only the engineering ones. This is where the volume is, and where a fresher without a specialist degree is genuinely competitive.

Set up alerts on three or four company sites rather than checking twenty occasionally. GCC roles fill from a much smaller applicant pool than a mass campus drive. Being early matters more than applying widely.

## Your first week: a checklist

1. Pick four companies from the list above that have a centre in a city you can reach.
2. Find each one's own careers page and set an alert for entry level roles in India.
3. Apply to every operations, analyst and associate role you are eligible for, not only the ones with "engineer" in the title.
4. Check [live openings in Hyderabad](/city/hyderabad) and [Bengaluru](/city/bengaluru) on this site, and follow each through to the employer's own page.

## Three mistakes freshers make applying to GCCs

Waiting for campus. GCCs hire far less through mass campus drives than IT services do. If you are waiting for them to visit, you will wait.

Only applying to roles with "AI" in the title. Half the AI hiring is operations work sitting under other titles. Applying only to the glamorous 49% is competing for the smaller half.

Paying anyone. No GCC, and no legitimate employer of any kind, charges a candidate a registration fee, a security deposit or a training charge to be considered. Anyone asking is not recruiting you.
